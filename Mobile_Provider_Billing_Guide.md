# Mobile Provider Billing Guide

## Allowances
- Phone allowance: the first 1,000 minutes in a billing month are free.
- Internet allowance: the first 20 GB (20,000 MB) in a billing month are free.
- 1 GB is 1,000 MB for this assignment.

## Extra Charges
- Extra phone usage is charged in started blocks of 1,000 minutes at $10 per block.
- Extra internet usage is charged in started blocks of 10 GB (10,000 MB) at $10 per block.

## Payment
- Payments are simulated. No card or real money is processed.
- Partial payment reduces the remaining balance.
- Reusing a transaction ID for the same or another payment is rejected.
- A payment cannot exceed the remaining balance.

## Example
- 35 GB internet usage produces $10 for the first 10 GB block after the 20 GB allowance.
- 1,001 phone minutes produce $10 of extra phone charges.
